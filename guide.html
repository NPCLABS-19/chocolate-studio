A continuous surface.

Draw a broad relief pattern, see it in chocolate, then wrap it in your artwork. Everything runs locally, without an image generation service.

Draw and mirror

Choose Draw, Line or a shape, then drag on the pattern canvas. Left/right and top/bottom mirror apply to each new form. Select an existing form to move it; its own mirror controls, scale, rotation and dimensions appear under Selected form. Mirrored copies remain linked.

Use B for Draw, V for Select, Delete to remove, ⌘ Z / Ctrl Z to undo. Arrow keys move selected forms by 0.1 mm; hold Shift for 1 mm. Undo also restores removed layers or replaced patterns.

Import SVG keeps outlines as a single editable form. Convert text, symbols and clipping effects to simple paths before importing. The importer keeps path geometry, local transforms and simple fill/stroke styles; scripts, images and external references are removed.

Relief and mould

The preview unions vector outlines and extrudes them into solid geometry, with large flat tops and adjustable bevel transitions. Relief is attached to the bar; there are no floating wires or portion divisions. Bar width, height, thickness, relief height and edge bevel use millimetres. Mould view shows the inverse pattern in a single cavity.

This is a visual design tool. A mould maker must confirm minimum feature widths, corner radii, draft, wall thickness, shrinkage, release and final tool geometry. SVG is positive face artwork, not a certified mould drawing or 3D manufacturing file.

Wrap and layer

Reveal a layer using its eye control. Select it to change material colour and roughness. Each wrapping layer covers the full bar; use its eye control to hide or reveal it. Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP, then adjust artwork scale, X/Y position and rotation. Artwork maps to the front face; side and back faces use the material colour. This version uses simple fitted shells, not print dielines.

Layers higher in the list sit farther from the chocolate. Move a selected layer outward or inward with the arrow controls. Add another foil, sleeve or carton, hide it or remove it. The Separate layers slider moves each wrapper forward to inspect the assembly. Bare bar and Wrapped provide quick visibility presets. The 3:6 control sets a 60 × 120 × 7.5 mm working bar. Source contours are fitted without stretching.

Save and export

  • PNG: 2400 × 2760 px, studio background included, without interface controls. Uses your current camera view.
  • SVG: visible positive pattern artwork, in millimetres. Its description records relief height and bevel.
  • Project: editable JSON containing all forms, symmetry, hidden layers, scene settings and embedded artwork. Open it to continue on another device.

The browser autosaves as you work. Use Save project for a portable backup, especially with large artwork. Uploaded images are normalized locally to a maximum 2400 px edge. No files are sent to a server.
