# Azura frame library

Current selected bar: 3:6, 60 × 120 × 7.5 mm.

- `traces/`: T01–T08 source-contour SVGs, separate broadened relief SVGs, 3:6 editable projects, and chocolate / inverse-cavity PNG renders. These preserve the visible scan contours; photography, occlusion and image resolution can affect accuracy. Raw contour files and broadened relief files are deliberately separate.
- `source-scans/` and `sources.json`: all 23 captured images from the supplied Pinterest board and their pin URLs.
- `svg/`, `centerlines/`, `projects/`, `renders/`: earlier A01–A10 interpretations at 4:6, retained as an archive. These are adaptations, not faithful traces.
- `azura-design-study.md`: the original detailed 23-pin design analysis. Its 4:6 translation section describes that earlier stage; the current working bar is 3:6.

In the live studio, wrappers are full cover with hide/show controls. Exported SVGs describe positive face artwork, not manufacturing-approved tooling CAD. A mould maker must check channel widths, draft and release. Source photo captures do not establish original authorship or geographic origin for every motif.

The separate terrain artwork set is available from `/terrain.html`. The selected five PNGs have opaque aquamarine-to-off-white blanket gradients, with no transparent water, underwater texture or ripple depiction in the open fields.
